Scanned the breakfast menu at your hotel yesterday? Find it again in one tap.

QRCache is a fast, simple QR code scanner that remembers every code you scan, so you never have to hunt for the same code twice.

<b>Scan in a snap</b>
• Point your camera and it scans instantly, with a gentle tap when it finds a code
• Scan from a photo or screenshot
• Flashlight for dark restaurants and hotel rooms
• Works offline. Codes are read on your phone.

<b>Your history, organized</b>
• Every scan is saved automatically, newest first
• Grouped by Today, Yesterday, This week and Earlier
• Search everything you've scanned
• Filter by favorites, links, Wi-Fi, text and more
• Rename codes so they're easy to find ("Hotel breakfast menu")
• Scanning the same code again moves it to the top instead of making a copy
• Swipe to favorite or delete, with Undo

<b>Does the right thing with every code</b>
• Links open in an in-app browser or your default browser
• Join Wi-Fi networks without typing the password
• Save contacts, add calendar events, open locations in Maps
• Call, text or email straight from the code
• Copy or share anything

<b>Private by design</b>
• Free, with no ads, no account and no tracking
• Your history stays on your phone and is kept out of cloud backups
• Export your history as JSON or CSV, and import it back any time
• Optional: name links after their web page title (off unless you turn it on)

<b>Feels at home on your phone</b>
• Clean, modern design with light and dark themes
• Smooth animations and haptics
• Works on Android 6.0 and newer

QRCache is open source: https://github.com/Eros-Byrne/QRCache
