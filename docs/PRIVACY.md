# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

QRCache is a free app. It is built to keep your data on your phone.

## What the app stores

- The QR codes and barcodes you scan, their titles, and the date and time you scanned them.
- Your app settings.

All of this is stored **only on your device**. We do not have a server and cannot see your history.

## Camera

The app uses the camera only to read QR codes and barcodes while the Scan screen is open. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere.

## Network

QRCache connects to the internet only when:

- You open a link from a scan.
- You turn on **Fetch page titles for links** in Settings (off by default). The app then loads each newly scanned web link once, over a secure connection, to read its page title. Nothing else is sent, and no cookies or account details are included.

## What we don't do

- No accounts.
- No ads.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No selling or sharing of data.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual scans in the app, use **Settings → Clear all history**, or uninstall the app. All data is removed from your device.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and noted in the app's release notes.
